# Sunset Over Ocean

## Description

A vibrant sunset captured during my residency at the coastal artist colony. This piece explores the interplay between warm and cool tones during the golden hour.

## Details

The composition draws the viewer's eye toward the horizon, where the sun dissolves into the sea. Layers of translucent glazes create depth and luminosity.

Started with an underpainting in burnt sienna, then built up layers over several sessions. The clouds were the most challenging part—getting that soft, glowing effect took multiple attempts.

This painting is a love letter to the fleeting magic of dusk, born from my time at the windswept coastal artist colony, where the sea and sky conspire to rewrite the rules of light. The canvas captures a sunset not as a mere backdrop, but as a living, breathing dialogue between fire and water—a moment suspended in amber.
The composition is a study in tension and harmony. The horizon, a razor-thin line where the sun surrenders to the ocean, acts as the gravitational center of the piece. Here, the celestial and the terrestrial blur into a shimmering mirage, their boundaries dissolved by the alchemy of golden hour. The viewer’s gaze is irresistibly drawn to this vanishing point, as if standing at the edge of the world, witnessing the day’s final, defiant sigh.
Technically, the work is a testament to patience and layering. It began with a bold underpainting of burnt sienna, a warm, earthy foundation that peeks through the subsequent strata like the memory of a summer’s day. Over this, I applied translucent glazes in thin, deliberate veils—each one a whisper of color that builds depth and luminosity. The effect is a canvas that seems to glow from within, as though lit by the sun’s last embers.
The clouds were the painting’s most stubborn muse. Rendering their ethereal softness required a dance between control and surrender. I reworked them time and again, blending and lifting pigment until they achieved that elusive quality: a glow that feels both ephemeral and eternal, like a half-remembered dream. The cool lavenders and peaches of the sky contrast with the deep, molten oranges and crimsons of the sea, creating a push-and-pull that animates the scene.
What emerges is more than a landscape; it’s an emotion. The painting invites you to linger in the quiet drama of transition—to feel the salt in the air, the hush of the waves, and the bittersweet ache of beauty that cannot last. It’s a reminder that even in stillness, there is movement, and in endings, there is always the promise of another beginning.
